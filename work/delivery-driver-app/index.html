PROJECT

Delivery Driver App

A Flutter and Firebase app that gives delivery drivers one place to manage their assigned deliveries, on iOS and Android.

  • Flutter
  • Firebase
  • MVVM
  • Secure sign-in and a live queue of assigned deliveries, synced in real time.
  • Navigation to each customer's location with maps integration.
  • Pickup and delivery confirmation by scanning and validating QR codes.
  • Push notifications, offline support and battery-conscious location use.
  • An MVVM architecture with a separate service layer.
