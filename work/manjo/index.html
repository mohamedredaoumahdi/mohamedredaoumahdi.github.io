PROJECT

Manjo

Client project (Denmark, 2025). A native iOS party game in Danish: friends pass one phone around and play through six game modes, including challenges, team games, question decks, Imposter and Bullshit.

  • Swift
  • SwiftUI
  • SpriteKit
  • StoreKit
  • MVVM

What I built

  • Card-based gameplay with spinning wheels and voting, for named players and teams.
  • Save and resume for games in progress.
  • An alcohol-free mode that filters the cards.
  • App Store review prompts at the right moment, using StoreKit.
  • SpriteKit particle effects and custom animations, on a SwiftUI and MVVM architecture.
